## Completed first fixed study

Run `python3 select_strategy.py` for reproducible pre-2021 selection, and `python3 validate_study.py` for arithmetic/chronology checks. `evaluate_once.py` refuses to overwrite its saved final result; the first final evaluation is preserved. The plan was written before this study’s selection and final replay, but authors had inspected these historical dates in older studies; no pristine test claim is made.

Selection compared a fixed 45-policy family: momentum, low volatility, mean/volatility ranking, twelve constant industry holdings, and equal weighting. The development score period is 2011–2016 (24 quarters), with 2009–2010 used only for initial history. Validation is 2017–2020 (16 quarters). Because the market series is withheld from strategy research, selection uses the cost-matched equal-weight industry proxy. Thus training/validation wins are not wins against the final market benchmark. This mismatch is a real limitation, not a claim of equivalent targets.

The selected rule is constant BusEq: 14/16 validation wins against the proxy, followed by 9/16 final wins against the market. Final equal weight wins 7/16; the best observed constant industry, Money, wins 10/16. The selected method therefore does not demonstrate a research advantage over static holdings. No model attempt or successful hard reference is claimed, no hurdle was lowered, and no final-period-driven retuning was performed.

`evidence/validation.json` records 135 comparisons between optimized feature selection and the actual interpreter, 1,005 exact chronological history checks with no pre-2009 rows, unchanged selected-source hash, and independent quarterly accounting for all twelve single-industry baselines. Full container execution remains untested. Scoring in a few quarters cannot establish generalizable skill; selecting a cutoff after viewing these results would be retrospective.

## Owner-approved benchmark disclosure and aligned-target study

Only 2009–2016 and 2017–2020 daily market returns were exported as development_market.csv and validation_market.csv. They align with every visible industry date; no post-2020 benchmark row was exported. `market-target-plan.json` fixes the unchanged 45-rule family and selection directly against actual market wins. `select_market.py` selects from visible data only and refuses to overwrite its record. The chosen policy is still constant BusEq, byte-identical to the earlier policy. Its prior 9/16 final result is explicitly reused, not portrayed as a new run. See evidence/market_target_selection.json and evidence/market_target_final_evaluation.json.

A separate bounded learned-family experiment was fixed in learned-plan.json before fitting. It uses eight trailing compound-return/volatility features and six ridge-score/top-k variants. Training features and quarter-end labels end by 2016; pooled scaling is fitted only on those training rows. Validation selection uses actual 2017–2020 market wins. Overlapping daily industry labels are dependent, so 16,968 rows are not 16,968 independent market episodes. The linear output is a ranking score, not a calibrated probability. This family’s selected model wins 10/16 validation quarters but only 4/16 final quarters, with mean final quarterly excess about -2.482 percentage points. No final-period retuning or new pass hurdle followed this failure. It was not the overall validation champion across all rule families.

Both study changes were made after authors had seen prior final-period outcomes. These are disclosed exploratory revisions, not independent blind tests. No reward, frontier-agent run, completed hard benchmark or future investment edge is claimed. `evidence/market_revision_validation.json` records the benchmark boundaries and thirty learned-feature/interpreter spot checks; existing chronology/accounting checks still apply. Current evidence does not establish a passing research-level reference.
